This is somewhat of reprise of last season's [Bull and Cinder](https://eat.zdlab.xyz/hotsauce/2025/10/20/bull_and_cinder/). I really liked the way that one turned out and the method used to get there- combine a fermented hot sauce with homemade barbecue sauce for extra complexity. While neither the Aji Chombo nor Chocolate Habanero technically rank as "superhots", this still sauce brings the heat.

Rather than try to match last year's flavor exactly, I gave this one its own character. The 3-week ferment contained a couple handfuls of dried figs from the tree in our backyard. The barbecue sauce was more aggrssively spiced and sweetened than last year's and bone broth was used in place of homemade stock. The result is a mix of sour lacto funk, subtle smokiness, and umami with enough sweetness to balance it all out.
